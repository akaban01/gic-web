Where your gift goes

Choosing a fund

Every method above lets you direct your gift. If you don’t specify one, it goes to general operations.

Sadaqa

Voluntary charity supporting the day-to-day running of the masjid.

Zakat

Obligatory almsgiving, distributed to eligible recipients.

Construction

Building, renovation, and expansion of the GIC campus.

Loan payoff

Retiring the loan on the 6.5-acre property.

Not sure which fund to choose, or want to set up a recurring gift? Email info@gicmasjid.org or call (512) 522-4595 and we’ll help you sort it out.

Loan payoff campaign

Help GIC become debt-free

GIC is working to pay off the loan on its 6.5-acre campus so more of every dollar donated goes directly to programs, not interest. Every gift toward the loan payoff brings the community closer to a debt-free masjid — a lasting gift, a sadaqah jāriyah.
