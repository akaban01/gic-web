    Friday prayer

    Jumu’ah at GIC

    Two khutbahs every Friday, so the community can pray around work and school. Unlike the daily prayers, these times stay the same all year.

    First Jumu’ah
    2:00 PM Khutbah
    2:30 PM Salah

    The earlier service — the busier of the two. Come a few minutes before the khutbah to find a spot.

    Second Jumu’ah
    3:00 PM Khutbah
    3:30 PM Salah

    A later option for anyone who cannot leave work or school in time for the first khutbah.

    Jumu’ah times as published by GIC. Attending for the first time? See what to expect on your first visit, or call (512) 522-4595 to confirm before you set out.

    Our mission

    Building the future together

    Georgetown Islamic Center is creating a strong foundation for Muslims in the Round Rock and Georgetown area — a place to strengthen faith, nurture families, and grow a lasting community.

    What began in 2018 with families gathering in homes and parks has grown into a 6.5-acre campus with a working masjid, weekly classes, and an expanding partnership with Darul Uloom Austin. Every part of that growth has been carried by the community it serves.

    Ways to give
    Educational partner

    Darul Uloom Austin (DUA)

    Darul Uloom Austin is an Islamic educational institution dedicated to teaching the Qur’an and Islamic studies. GIC serves as one of its three campuses, alongside NAMCC and ICBC.

    Visit DUA website Programs at GIC

    Growing together

    Sixteen years of graduates, and counting

    Since 2007, DUA has expanded to include full-time boys’ and girls’ programs, Naseeha seminary tracks, Deen Intensives, and academic tracks at GIC. In 2025 alone, DUA celebrated its 16th graduation, with 28 alumni now serving as scholars, educators, and professionals.

    In times of loss

    Funeral support

    For burial coordination, contact the ICGA Muslim Cemetery during business hours. GIC can help arrange Ghusl (ritual washing) and Janazah (funeral prayer) — reach out to us directly for assistance.
