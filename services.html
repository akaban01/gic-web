Funeral services

Supporting families in times of loss

Losing a loved one is one of life’s most difficult moments. We help our community navigate the Islamic burial process with care and dignity, coordinating with the ICGA Muslim Cemetery to ensure your loved one is honored according to Islamic tradition.

When a death occurs

  1. Recite Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji’un — “Verily, to Allah we belong and to Him we shall return.”

  2. Contact the ICGA Cemetery line during business hours at (512) 666-3389.

  3. Fill out the Burial Intake Form on the ICGA Cemetery website. They will contact you to coordinate next steps.

  4. GIC can help coordinate Ghusl (ritual washing) and Janazah (funeral prayer). Contact us directly at info@gicmasjid.org for assistance.

Marriage services

Nikkah & marriage services

GIC is honored to perform Nikkah (Islamic marriage) ceremonies for members of our community. Our Imam will guide you through the process in accordance with Islamic principles and Texas state law.

Requirements

  • The bride and groom must both be Muslim. If one is not, he/she must be willing to take the Shahadah before the Nikkah ceremony begins.
  • A legal marriage certificate from the county must be obtained in advance.

Submit a Nikkah request

Email us to begin the process. Our team will follow up to confirm availability and walk you through next steps.
