What to expect on your first visit

Dress modestly

Loose, modest clothing is appreciated. Headscarves are available for sisters who would like one.

Shoes off

Please remove your shoes before entering the prayer hall — shelving is provided at the entrance.

Ask questions

Someone is almost always around before and after prayer — don’t hesitate to introduce yourself.
