Education at GIC

Learning for every age

Georgetown Islamic Center hosts diverse educational programs for all ages, from Qur’anic studies to advanced seminary tracks.

Programs

Qur’an, seminary, and Sunday School

Qur’an Programs

Full-time Hifz, After School, and Summer Programs for boys and girls, ages 6–15.

Held Monday–Thursday, 5:00–7:00 PM. Scholarships available.

Seminary Programs

The Naseeha Program and a Full-time Seminary track for students pursuing deeper Islamic scholarship.

Al Iman Sunday School

Weekly Islamic education for children — Qur’an, akhlaq, and the foundations of faith.

Interested in enrolling? Email info@gicmasjid.org or call (512) 522-4595 for current schedules, tuition, and availability.

Educational partner

Darul Uloom Austin (DUA)

A 501(c)(3) non-profit institution preparing future leaders with sound Islamic knowledge rooted in the Qur’an and Sunnah.

Over the years, DUA has grown to operate multiple campuses — including NAMCC, ICBC, and GIC — offering full-time and part-time Hifdh, After School, and Seminary programs, powered by dedicated teachers, experienced scholars, and a nurturing community.

Starting in 2007, DUA has since expanded to include girls’ and boys’ full-time programs, Naseeha seminary programs, Deen Intensives, and academic tracks at GIC. In 2025, DUA celebrated its 16th graduation, with 28 alumni now serving in roles from Islamic scholarship to industry and higher education.
