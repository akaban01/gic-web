New to Islam

New Muslim resources

Anyone who has recently accepted Islam — or is simply curious — is welcome to reach out, one on one.

A New Muslim Resource Guide is available on request, and the Imam is glad to meet in person to answer questions, walk through the basics of prayer, and introduce you to the community.

Request the guide Meet the Imam

You will not be doing this alone

  • Someone to sit with you and answer questions, at your pace
  • A printed guide covering prayer, wudu, and the fundamentals
  • An introduction to families in the community
  • Classes you can join whenever you are ready
